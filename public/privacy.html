Privacy & site data

Updated: 13 October 2026

This notice describes the technical data use of the NickolasSpace website and the contact options linked from it.

Language preference

The website stores your selected language in your browser's local storage under nickolas-language. This preference is read on your device and is not sent by the website to a server. You can remove it by clearing this site's browser data. The website continues to work if storage is unavailable.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Requests to load pages and files include connection information such as an IP address. GitHub describes its data processing in its privacy statement.

The homepage loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which requires your browser to contact Google's font servers. See Google Fonts privacy information.

Analytics and contact links

The website does not include an advertising pixel, an analytics script or an on-site enquiry form. Contact buttons open email or external messaging services. The privacy terms of those services apply when you use them.

If you send a message, its contents and the contact details you provide are available to the recipient through the chosen service. Share only the information needed to discuss your project.

Questions about your data

For questions about this website or information you have shared with NickolasSpace, contact kkosmacevskij@gmail.com. You can also use this address to request information about, correction of or deletion of your correspondence.
