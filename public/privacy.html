Privacy & site data

Updated: 13 October 2026

This notice describes the current website and its contact options. NickolasSpace is a project operated by Mykola Kosmachevskyi, Ukraine. For website or data enquiries, email kkosmacevskij@gmail.com.

Language preference

Only the language you select is saved under nickolas-language in this browser's local storage. It stays on your device until you clear it or the browser removes it. The website does not send this preference to a server and continues to work if storage is blocked.

Hosting, fonts and media

GitHub Pages delivers the website. Requests include connection information such as your IP address and browser details. GitHub may process technical logs under its privacy statement. Its retention and processing are controlled by GitHub, not by this site's browser code.

Fonts, images and videos are served with the website. Viewing this page does not load fonts from Google or embed social media services.

Analytics and messages

This website has no advertising pixels, analytics scripts, login or on-site enquiry submission. It does not set application cookies. Language storage is used only to remember your explicit selection.

Contact options open your email application or an external service. Opening the contact dialog does not send a message. If you contact us, your message and provided contact details are used to respond and discuss your request. Email is received through Gmail; Instagram and Telegram contacts use those services. Their privacy terms apply. Do not send sensitive information that is unnecessary for your enquiry.

Information still being finalised

NickolasSpace is preparing its operating arrangements. Detailed legal bases and retention periods for correspondence, postal contact details, and arrangements concerning service providers and international transfers still need to be documented. This technical notice is not a complete statement of GDPR compliance. Contact the email above about the handling of a specific message.

Your data questions and rights

You may ask about data held in correspondence and request access, correction, deletion, restriction or, where applicable, portability or object to processing. Rights depend on the circumstances and applicable law. You may also complain to a competent data protection authority, such as IMY in Sweden if relevant to you.

If optional analytics or advertising technologies are introduced, this notice and the consent requirements must be reviewed before they are enabled.
